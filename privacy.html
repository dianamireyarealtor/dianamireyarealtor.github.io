Privacy Policy

Creative Studio · Effective October 28, 2026

Creative Studio is a private video-production application used only by Diana Mireya Realtor and her team. This policy explains what Google user data it accesses and how that data is used.

Google user data accessed

Creative Studio requests a single Google permission, the Google Drive scope drive.file. Google describes it as permission to "see, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app". Creative Studio uses it only with:

  • the Drive folder the account owner selects when connecting Google Drive, and
  • the files and folders Creative Studio itself creates in that folder.

Creative Studio does not request access to Gmail, contacts, calendar or any other Google service, or to the owner's name, email address or profile.

How the data is used

  • To save copies of the owner's original video footage into the owner's own Google Drive.
  • To read back the stored file's name, size and checksum, to confirm that each copy is complete and unchanged.

Storage and sharing

  • Videos uploaded through Creative Studio stay in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's own computer.
  • The authorization Google issues is stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted for the owner's account.
  • Creative Studio does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.
  • Data obtained through Google APIs is not used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

Creative Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

The owner can remove Creative Studio's access to Google Drive at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Creative Studio does not automatically delete original footage from Google Drive; saved files stay there until the owner removes them.
